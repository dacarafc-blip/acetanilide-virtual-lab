Experiment 3: Acetanilide Synthesis

Session: ACET-2026-0000
Learning Outcomes
  • Synthesize acetanilide via nucleophilic acyl substitution of aniline with acetic anhydride.
  • Purify crude product via in-situ aqueous recrystallization and calculate percentage yield.
  • Verify purity through qualitative functional-group tests and capillary melting point determination (113–115 °C).
Reagents & Apparatus

Aniline (10 drops, ~0.50 g), Acetic Anhydride (20 drops, ~1.08 g), Distilled Water (2 × 5.0 mL + ice-cold rinse), 10 mL Graduated Cylinder, Dedicated Reagent Pipettes (PIP. A & B), Analytical Balance (±0.0001 g), Hot Water Bath, Ice Bath, Gravity Funnel, Melting Point Apparatus.

Step 1: Tare & Weigh 10 Drops of Aniline Active

Drag the test tube onto the Analytical Balance, click TARE, then drag PIP. A over the tube (or use buttons) to dispense 10 drops of aniline.

💡 Chemistry Rationale: Taring the empty tube first eliminates systematic positive error so you weigh only pure aniline (C₆H₅NH₂), the limiting reagent in your stoichiometric calculation.
Step 2: Measure 5.0 mL H₂O & Add 20 Drops Acetic Anhydride Pending

Fill the 10 mL Graduated Cylinder with 5.0 mL distilled water, drag it onto the test tube to pour, then add 20 drops of acetic anhydride via PIP. B.

💡 Chemistry Rationale: Aqueous medium disperses aniline droplets and moderates reaction heat while excess acetic anhydride (~2:1 molar ratio) drives complete N-acetylation without diacetylating the amine.
Step 3: Stir & Precipitate Crude Acetanilide (5 min) Pending

Stir continuously for 5 minutes until white crude acetanilide precipitates out of solution.

💡 Chemistry Rationale: Because acetanilide is sparingly soluble in room-temperature water (~0.54 g/100 mL), sustained agitation promotes nucleation and precipitates crude white crystals.
Step 4: In-Situ Recrystallization (Hot Water Bath) Pending

Measure a second 5.0 mL distilled water in the cylinder, pour into the tube, drag tube to the Hot Water Bath, turn the Stove ON, and heat until dissolved.

💡 Chemistry Rationale: Acetanilide exhibits a steep solubility curve—dissolving readily near 90–100 °C (~5.5 g/100 mL) to release trapped acetic acid and unreacted aniline into the mother liquor.
Step 5: Two-Stage Slow Cooling (RT Stand → Ice Bath) Pending

Drag the hot tube to the RT Stand first to nucleate large crystals, then drag to the Ice Bath (0–4 °C) to maximize recovery.

💡 Chemistry Rationale: Cooling gradually to 25 °C before chilling at 0–4 °C prevents oiling-out and occlusion of impurities inside rapidly formed crystal lattices.
Step 6: Gravity Filtration, Ice-Cold Rinse & Drying Pending

Weigh empty filter paper, place it in the funnel, drag tube to pour & filter, rinse with ice-cold water, dry, and weigh final product.

💡 Chemistry Rationale: Rinsing with ice-cold (0–4 °C) water washes away soluble acetic acid without redissolving your purified acetanilide crystals.
